# College Tech Club: Week 5 (CSS Fundamentals)

## Why these colours
1. The warm off-white background (#F4F1EA) with dark ink text (#1F2A33) is easy on the eyes and gives strong contrast for reading.
2. Deep teal (#24454A) gives the club a calm, serious identity and is used for the header, hero and table heading.
3. A single ochre accent (#A35A17) is used only for links, focus outlines and hover states, so it guides attention without making the page busy.
