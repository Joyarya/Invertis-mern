WEEK 6: Profile Card
Track: Box Model, Display & Positioning (PRACTICAL LAB 2)
Concepts: box-sizing, padding, border, margin, border-radius, overflow, sticky vs fixed, z-index

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Understand the box model and position elements on screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM STATEMENT (FINAL QUESTION / PROJECT GOAL):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Make a profile card with a round photo circle and a scrolling bio box. Add a top
bar that stays on screen and a round + button that floats at the bottom.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASKS (COVERS 6.1 TO 6.5):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Use box-sizing: border-box. Play with padding, border and margin. Take a DevTools screenshot and label the box model.
6.2 Make the card with a border, round corners and a circle avatar. Then copy it to make 2 colour versions.
6.3 Give the bio box a fixed height and overflow: auto so it scrolls.
6.4 Make the top bar sticky and the + button fixed. Use z-index.
6.5 Clean up your class names, test it, and submit.
